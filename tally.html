07. Beware the Debater! 21/10/26
Beware the debater! Every day of your life, no matter where you are, no matter what time it is, no matter what you're doing, he's there and he's ready to argue about nothing.

I have a few friends who are very... assertive. I'm not really a political person, I mean I have my opinions on issues but I don't actively spread them with anyone. But a select few people are so passionate about politics and they'll stand their ground so strongly, trying to sway them is like trying to sway a building. It's not gonna happen.

But I feel this political passion comes across in apolitical conversations too. It's not too often when I slip up and say something I know the other person disagrees with and have to watch them completely dismantle my view. The gunshot is a stern "no," and the pain is the seriousness of their stance which they carefully lay out to me as if it's my very first day on earth.

Of course these are once in a blue moon, but I do find it weird that some people are like this. But...

...is it just some people?

I've been described as "arguing for the sake of arguing," which I think is harsh in the moment but then later realise is pretty accurate, and then I go back to arguing. Before I make this about myself (again), I want to get to the point of this post since this one's gonna be long and pretty empty. I would make it shorter so it's easier for readers to read, but there are no readers. Not here at least.

We all debate over things. This or that, X or Y, 1 or 2. But we really should practice not caring as much. Leave our minds to worry about the important issues. The other shit does not matter as much.

An example for me is this website. I really want to change it's looks but I'm conflicted between what I want for it. One idea was the previously mentioned map with all of the different pages being different places. Did I mention that part? Probably not.

Another is something like a mix of Spinebreaker's amazing website and the awesome nonograms.org. A bunch of colour vomit on the sides and top and all of the important content in the middle, with widgets and stuff reserved for the sides. I realise that description isn't too flattering, the idea is a lot nicer in my head (I hope).

Or maybe the way the site looks right now is fine! The 3D simplistic look seems pretty unique, and I wouldn't want to pander to being "nostalgic" or "retro" if I didn't actually like how it looked.

I guess the point is that this doesn't really matter at the end of the day. There are way bigger fish to fry, like getting better at animating - I'm still doing the 5 animations thing, I thing writing blogs are fine since they're so quick to make. Also I think a good comprimise between making stories and making actions is making bumpers! Kind of like the credits that play after watching all three (as of now) animations in TV Mode, but obviously more... more.

Here's another example of a pointless debate. One time when I was watching TV one of those charity foundation group ads came on. It had a bunch of students in proper uniform and one other kid in a faded unbranded uniform.

Since I'm trying to feel anything else but guilt I think "this kid is being used for this ad. If they paid him, as they should, then this kid isn't an example of a poor kid anymore. If they didn't pay him or paid him poorly then the foundation are douches. And if the kid isn't even poor and just an actor then this whole thing is just fake advertising." I thought this a good while back and only remembered it recently. Obviously the issue with this is that it's ignoring the actual issue, pretty much just a whataboutism.

Here's another. I've began watching Jojo's Bizarre Adventure and one thing I like about it a lot admist all the other cool shit is the music in it. Part 1 and 2 use Yes' Roundabout, a song which I love a lot and one I though fit very well. Then in Part 3 they use some other song in the first few episodes, then use Pat Metheny's Last Train Home. Initially I thought that Roundabout would've surely been better. It's understandable that they probably couldn't use it again, but were there no other songs? What about Heart of the Sunrise or Siberian Khatru also by Yes?

But after watching more and nearing the end of the part I grew accustomed to it. It wasn't the song I wanted (bias be damned I guess) but it actually did fit quite well. When I finished Part 3 I searched up the song and listened to it in full, and I was blown away. The song is remarkable, especially the bit near the end where the voices come in.

I guess that's less of a debate than the others, but it's moreso "arguing for the sake of arguing," or "disliking for the sake of disliking" is probably more apt.

One more example. For the release of CTC I was going to make an animation with everyone's favourite random assortment of characters. I made music for it and I had a pretty good script for it. The ending bit of the music is reused in the TV Mode credits mentioned before.

But I also needed voice actors, and given that I'm a tightass the only option was doing the voices myself. I spend about one hour trying to do the first line and I give up. I'm what you'd call "not a fan" of hearing my own voice.

So the opportunity was missed, but I eventually had another idea. An animation of a CTC afterparty, everyone celebrating the release of the book, except Supreno who was stuck at home being sick. That's the context behind this image.



Everything was going great. I made the characters and gave them all formal variants. I edited the Hub Nebula bryce scene to make it look a bit more party-esc. I made the script, it's looking fabulous.

And then I needed to voice act. God knows I'm not doing the voices myself so my options are TTS edited to sound as human as I can get it, or some sort of Undertale style speaking. I'm sure it has a name but I don't even know what to guess.

I tried the later. I took random samples, pitched them and bitcrushed them. I tried making the first line for Moon Float and...



"Give up! Please! You know you can’t take another shot!"


Yea, I haven't touched it since. Maybe it was the toxic combo of realising that the line was actually ass and realising that the delivery was auditory vomit.

I still think that the idea is good. If I get better at voice acting or find people I'll definitely revisit it. But as it stands now it's lost media, wooooooooo!!! Or I suppose just a lost idea... booooooooo...

I'll reiterate it again. Nothing I've mentioned here is actually worth caring about. The semantics of an ad, or potential web designs, it just doesn't matter. You shouldn't waste time trying to decide which idea is better if it's not gonna work out anyway.

Now that I think about this, the only example that that really applies to was the first one, the one about my website - this makes 3/7 pages about the website's design change by the way!

Oh well, I guess the real moral is to not worry about inconsequential stuff.

To leave you with an actual fitting example, that CTC party animation I was talking about had Jami down an entire whiskey bottle to beat onreGrid at a drinking game. If there's anyone listening, please don't ask.

I was thinking on whether to include this in her character bio. It's a bit of a quirky fact, but it's also kind of cynical. "My superpower is that I can drink a lot" isn't very wholesome chungus as it were.

I decided to exclude it since it was a pretty jarring bit of info, plus I'd already written a lot for her. But in the end, as mentioned in the last post, the character bio page comes out when the website gets redesigned. So it was all for not! Trivial things should not eat away at your mind. Sometimes a spontaneous decision that gets things moving is better than being halted indefinitely due to being cursed by the debater spirit within you.

Thanks for reading! If you actually did read all of this, first of all you're only 4 more smorgasbords away from earning yourself a nifty little prize! And secondly I am truly sorry.
